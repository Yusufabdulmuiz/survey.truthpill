According to NAFDAC, up to 15% of circulating medicines in Nigeria are substandard or falsified. Counterfeiters seamlessly clone the packaging of routine medications for malaria, diabetes, blood pressure, and other chronic ailments.

When you unknowingly swallow these counterfeit pills, you are not just missing the cure. You are ingesting heavy metals, chalk, or expired chemicals that cause irreversible kidney damage, liver failure, and death.
